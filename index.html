Scan this with your phone's camera to open this exact page on your phone, then use the Photo/Video controls above as normal — captured files download to your phone.

For the phone's camera to work, this page must be loaded over https:// — a plain file:// path or a local http:// network address usually won't be allowed by the phone's browser. If you're serving it from your PC (e.g. python3 -m http.server), tools like ngrok or Tailscale Funnel can give you a temporary https:// link to point the QR code at.

Runs entirely in your browser via getUserMedia — no upload, no server. Works best in Chrome, Firefox, or Edge over HTTPS (or localhost).
